## Context

A reviewer rejects a change and writes why — in a review decision. The person
who has to act on it works in the margin. Today those are two disconnected
records, and the comment thread that prompted the rejection has no idea it
was answered.

## Scope

- **Link, in both directions, additively:**
  - an optional `review_decision_ref` on the thread (decision id + the
    object's `content_revision` at decision time) recording that a decision
    answered this thread;
  - a comment posted **as part of** a review decision, authored by the
    deciding principal, carrying the decision's reason text, so the thread
    reads as *asked → decided*.
- **The rail surfaces it:** a linked thread shows a decision chip
  (`approved` / `changes requested`, tone from the existing badge
  vocabulary), and the decision's reason renders as the thread's newest
  comment.
- **Threads do not auto-resolve on a decision.** A decision answers a thread;
  a human closes it. This is the §8.2 rule and the point where it is most
  tempting to break — do not.
- **Where the link is made:** submitting a review decision from a surface
  that has thread context (the object workspace's Comments tab, and the rail
  once a thread is pinned) offers "answer these open threads", listing the
  object's open threads with checkboxes. Nothing happens implicitly to
  threads the reviewer did not name.
- **Attention accounting:** a thread answered by a decision but not resolved
  still counts in `Attention N` — it still needs someone. Its badge gains the
  decision chip, not a decrement.
- **Unit tests (`node:test`):** the link recorded on both sides for a
  decision naming two of three open threads (the third untouched); no
  auto-resolution; a decision on an object with zero threads is a no-op; and
  the render-order rule that the decision comment is last.

## Acceptance criteria

- Deciding a review with two threads named produces two threads carrying the
  decision reference and the reason as their newest comment, both still
  `open`.
- The unnamed third thread is byte-identical to before.
- `Attention N` is unchanged by a decision alone.
- The link is visible in both the rail and the admin Comments tab.
- `npm run check` and `npm test` green. Committed `T17.12: …`; no PR.

## Non-goals

No change to `review_decide`'s own semantics, gating, or state machine. No
blocking a publish on open threads (a policy decision nobody has made). No
auto-resolve, ever. No new review states.
