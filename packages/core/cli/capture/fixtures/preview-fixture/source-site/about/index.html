How we work

Manuscripts arrive by post. We read them away from a screen, answer every one, and take on two a year. An accepted book is edited over eight months, set in metal for the title pages, and printed in runs of nine hundred.

Nothing is reprinted. When a run is gone the book stays gone, which keeps the shelf honest and the workshop quiet.

Where to find us

Booksellers who stock Larkmoor, and the two markets we attend each year.
