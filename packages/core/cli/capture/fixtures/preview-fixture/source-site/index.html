Our founder

Larkmoor was started by a printer who spent thirty years setting other people's sentences and wanted, once, to set her own. She still reads every manuscript twice: first walking, then sitting down.

The press keeps to two titles a year so that each one can be edited slowly, printed carefully, and sent out with a letter rather than a leaflet.
